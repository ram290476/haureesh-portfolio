I'm a senior at Dougherty Valley High School in San Ramon, California. I want to be an engineer. My intended majors are Physics, Aerospace, Mechanical, CS, Data Science, and Electrical.

Outside class I play cello in the DVHS Chamber Orchestra and have played piano since I was young. In 10th grade I served on Orchestra Council (social media, merch, marketing). I wrestle for DVHS, on JV and varsity, and I like helping newer teammates with technique. I help teach at the martial arts studio where I earned my first-degree black belt on October 24, 2020 and my second-degree black belt on April 29, 2023, after training from kindergarten on.

I tutor part-time at Best Brains Learning Centers. Last summer I ran my own small math tutoring group and donated the earnings. I've mentored younger students through Vibha since 2023, and I tutor my younger brother at home.

Bouncing back

My junior year was hard. I broke my hand and needed surgery, then I was hospitalized for rhabdomyolysis. I caught up on my classes and kept training. When a math grade slipped, I got tutoring and brought it back up to an A.
