Kids for Classrooms – RC Puram

In 2026 I was on the Bay Area team of the Vibha Youth Action Center campaign "Kids for Classrooms – Tech for RC Puram Government School" in Telangana, India, with Neetu Kulshrestha, Kamran Yunus, Veer Nayak and Vividh Sriram. Students and mentors brought together 50+ supporters. We provided projectors and large screens, a sound system, a multi-function printer and 4 laptops.

The school operates out of a makeshift community center, with about 700 students sharing 4–5 rooms. It had missed the textbook distribution deadline, so the digital resources gave students on-demand learning content. The campaign was part of the Vibha Youth Empowerment Committee's six-month effort across Bay Area, Minnesota (Grade 1 worksheets) and Chicago (learning videos) teams.

Source: Vibha, "Youth Empowerment in Action" by Vasudha Sriram, July 13, 2026.
